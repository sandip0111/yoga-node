Namaste,

If you have been practising Ashtanga Yoga, I want to ask you one simple question.

Are you practising Ashtanga Yoga… or Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga?

Most practitioners use these names interchangeably.

But according to the classical yogic tradition, they are not the same.

This is one of the most common misunderstandings in modern yoga.

In my latest article, I explore:

  • What Maharishi Patanjali actually meant by Ashtanga Yoga
  • Why Ashtanga Vinyasa developed through the Mysore tradition is different
  • What the Yoga Sutras really say about Asana
  • Why understanding this distinction can completely change the way you approach your practice

This article is not written to criticize any tradition.

It is written to bring us back to the original intention of yoga and the wisdom of the classical texts.

If you are a sincere practitioner, yoga teacher, or someone who wants to understand yoga beyond physical postures, I believe you will find this perspective valuable.
