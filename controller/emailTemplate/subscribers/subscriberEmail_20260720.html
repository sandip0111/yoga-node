Dear Yogi,

Have you ever wondered why traditional Ashtanga Vinyasa asks you to breathe through every posture?

Perhaps the greatest benefit of the practice isn’t flexibility or strength at all.

It is the cultivation of vitality.

Every posture places your breath in a new challenge. Every vinyasa teaches your lungs to remain calm under changing conditions. Slowly, the breath becomes longer, subtler, and more conscious—the very qualities Patanjali describes as the foundation for Pranayama.

When the breath becomes refined, the mind becomes steady.

When the mind becomes steady, your awareness naturally turns inward.

This is why the Primary Series is much more than a sequence of postures. It is a systematic training of the breath, preparing your body, mind, and pranic system for deeper yogic practices.
