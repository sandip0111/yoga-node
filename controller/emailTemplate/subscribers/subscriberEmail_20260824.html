You Don’t Breathe Through Both Nostrils All the Time. Here’s Why.

Have you ever noticed that one nostril feels more open than the other?

And then, after some time, the other nostril becomes more open?

Many people ask me:

“Why is one of my nostrils blocked?”

“Why does my breathing keep changing?”

The answer is more interesting than you might think.

🌬️ Your breath is constantly changing

You are not necessarily breathing equally through both nostrils all the time. In fact, the dominance of airflow naturally shifts between the two nostrils.

And Yoga has been observing this phenomenon for centuries.

☀️ The two nostrils & two energies

In the yogic tradition:

Left Nostril

Lunar principle → Moon 🌙

 

Right Nostril

Solar principle → Sun ☀️

These are not simply two physical passages for air. They are also understood in Yoga as expressions of different energetic currents within the system.

👀 Your breath can reveal a lot

Your breath is extremely sensitive to what is happening within you. You may notice changes in your breathing when you are:

  • stressed
  • anxious
  • tired
  • emotionally disturbed
  • physically unwell
  • or experiencing changes in your internal state

This is why traditional Yoga gives such importance to observing the breath.

Before you try to control the breath, learn to watch it.

🔁 What if one nostril keeps changing? How to balance

🧘‍♂️ Try this simple practice

  1. Sit comfortably.
    Keep the spine relaxed and upright.
  2. Close your eyes.
    Bring your attention to your blocked nose and breathe with speed (not worry, aggressive).
  3. Exhale from the open nose.

✓ Practice 3–5 times

Do this regularly, and you may begin to notice something fascinating:

With time and everyday practice, balance will come.

One important thing…

If one nostril remains persistently blocked, or you have pain or difficulty breathing, don’t assume it is only an energetic imbalance.

Explore the physical cause too.

Yoga teaches us to work with the whole human being — not to ignore the physical body.

Let’s practice this technique and stay healthy.
