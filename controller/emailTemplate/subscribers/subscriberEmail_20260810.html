What You Will Discover

During this webinar, I will share the traditional understanding of how sincere practice naturally matures into selfless service (Teaching), and how a Yoga Practitioner can become a Yoga Teacher.

Together we will discuss:

  • The true meaning of Sadhana (The Practice) and why it is the foundation of every authentic yoga teaching.
  • How sincere practice naturally evolves into Service (from self Practice to transforming teachings).
  • The qualities of a good yoga teacher beyond poses and certifications.
  • Why teaching begins with inner transformation rather than techniques.
  • The difference between traditional yoga and much of what is presented as modern yoga today.
  • How an authentic lineage preserves the depth and power of yoga.
  • Whether the path of teaching may be the next step in your own journey.

Whether you are considering a Yoga Teacher Training, already teaching, or simply seeking a deeper understanding of yoga, this session will offer a traditional perspective that can transform the way you relate to your practice.

Yoga is not simply something we do for an hour each day.

When practice becomes sincere, it naturally begins to express itself through the way we live, the way we serve, and the way we share with others.
