Prashant here. I want to tell you a very important insight about spiritual practices.

Do you know what is the first spiritual practice?

The first spiritual practice is not a technique. It is not a kriya. It is not a ritual.

"The first spiritual practice… is not to get sick."

When we begin spiritual sadhana — whether it is yoga, meditation, mantra, or any path — most of our effort goes into perfecting techniques.

We focus on:

  • How to sit correctly
  • How to breathe correctly
  • How to chant correctly
  • How to perform rituals correctly

But this is not where practice should begin. Practice must begin with a strong physical foundation.

And what is that foundation?

The body.

This beautiful body given by nature
which breathes without asking you,
which digests without instruction,
which heals without your awareness,
which allows you to experience life.

This body must be protected.

Join me every day, live for regular online yoga sadhana

If the body is disturbed, your meditation will be disturbed. If digestion is disturbed, your mind will be disturbed. If sleep is disturbed, your prana will be disturbed.

So the first sadhana is simple:

Design your life in a way that your body does not suffer.

Sit and reflect:

  • How should your day unfold?
  • Do you wake up aligned with the sun?
  • Do you eat sāttvic, clean food?
  • Do you take care of your bowel so daily purification happens naturally?
  • Do you protect your energy from draining environments?
  • Do you stay around people who elevate your consciousness?

And at night, before sleeping, ask yourself:

"Did my day unfold with awareness?

Or did I waste my prana?"

When your daily rhythm becomes harmonious… when your sleep, digestion, movement, and breath are stable… Then your foundation is ready.

Only then begin advanced techniques.

Otherwise, you are trying to build a temple on weak ground.

Spirituality is not escape from the body. It begins with honoring the body.

Protect your health.

Stabilize your routine.

Align with nature.

This is the first initiation.

Everything else comes later.
