What we will explore

Mysore is not chosen by accident. It is the city where Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga was systematized and taught for generations.

Practicing there carries a different quality - the tradition is in the air, in the streets, in the rhythm of the place itself.

Over these five days, we will work through the complete path of yoga as a living system, not as a collection of techniques, but as a coherent discipline that transforms how you move, breathe, and perceive.

  • Ashtanga Vinyasa Yoga — Led class. To understand the respiratory and energetic intelligence embedded in the sequence.
  • Pranayama — The bridge between the physical and the subtle.
  • Yoga Philosophy — Daily discussions on the traditional teachings that give the practice its direction and depth.
  • Meditation — Evening practice. Stillness as a condition, not an escape.
  • Temple Visit — On September 17 — a direct encounter with the living tradition that shaped this path.

Who this retreat is for

This retreat is for sincere practitioners. You do not need to be an advanced Ashtanga yogi. But you do need to come with the intention to work - not to collect experiences, but to deepen your understanding of what you are already doing.

If you practice Ashtanga and want to understand it from the inside - not just as a sequence of postures but as a complete energetic system - this is for you.

If you practice yoga in any form and sense that there is something more precise and traditional available to you - this is also for you.

These five days are a rare opportunity to work directly with the teacher in an intimate setting, in the city where the practice has lived for generations.
