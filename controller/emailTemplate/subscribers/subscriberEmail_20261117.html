Namaste,

Have you ever woken up in the morning, knowing you should practise yoga, but simply not felt like getting out of bed?

You open your eyes, but you don’t feel like moving. You reach for your phone, start scrolling, and somehow convince yourself to stay in bed a little longer.

Don’t worry. This happens to almost every practitioner.

But have you ever wondered why?

Sometimes, it is not simply laziness. It is also about understanding the energy that influences our actions, motivation and state of mind.

Here is a small yogic experiment I want you to try for the next 21 days.

A 5-minute morning Sadhana

Before getting out of bed:

1. Sit quietly for five minutes.

Keep your eyes closed and observe your natural breathing.

2. Observe your nostrils.

Notice which nostril has a stronger flow of breath: the right or the left.

3. Understand the connection with Surya Energy. SUN

In traditional Hatha Yoga, the right nostril is associated with Pingala Nadi, the solar channel, traditionally connected with activity, vitality and outward-directed energy.

When you feel dull or inactive, gently observe your right nostril breathing. If appropriate for your practice, try a few gentle breaths through the right nostril without forcing or speeding up the breath.

4. Observe the difference.

Notice how your body, mind and willingness to begin the day feel afterwards.

Don’t expect a miracle. Simply observe yourself.

Practise this experiment consistently for 21 days and become a witness to your own experience.

This is how Yoga becomes a part of your life.

Yoga is not limited to asanas, pranayama or meditation sessions. It is a way of understanding yourself, your energy, your mind and your actions.

You don’t always need more motivation. Sometimes, you need a deeper understanding of yourself.
