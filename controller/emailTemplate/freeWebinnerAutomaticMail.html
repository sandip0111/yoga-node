What You Will Discover

Monday, September 21st

📖 History of Yoga - Prashant

On our first day the founder of our school will lead us from tradition to modern practice.

Everyone practices yoga and follows a certain tradition but how many of us actually know where that tradition came from?
How yoga evolve?
Where did the different systems of yoga come from?
And how did the yoga we practice today develop?

We will go beyond simply practicing yoga. We will explore the origins, history, and evolution of yoga, and understand how different traditions and practices developed over time.

Tuesday, September 22nd

🧘 Hatha Yoga - Shivam

On our second day our traditional hatha teacher will lead a class for us.

Shivam has studied and practiced yoga in traditional ashrams in Rishikesh, and he has also train personally under Prashant Jakhmola Ji.

He is a government-certified Yoga Therapist under the Ministry of AYUSH (Ministry of Ayurveda, Yoga and Naturopathy, Unani, Siddha and Homoeopathy), India.

His approach combines traditional yogic knowledge with precise alignment and therapeutic application.

Wednesday, September 23rd

🌬️ Pranayama As It Is - Archana

On our third day, our Yogini from Rishikesh will lead a Pranayama class for us.

Archana´s wider studies include Nāda Yoga (Sound Healing), Yin Yoga, and Pre-Natal and Post-Natal Yoga. She has also been fortunate to learn from traditional masters including Swami Taponidhi of Bihar School of Yoga and Swami Ji Kapri, who worked closely with Swami Satyananda Saraswati Ji.

She is particularly devoted to exploring and sharing traditional approaches to Prāṇāyāma, Prāṇa Śakti, Dhyāna, Haṭha Yoga, Mantra, and Kuṇḍalinī.

Thursday, September 24th

🕉️ Yogic Living - Prashantji

On our fourth day we will have a class to take a deeper look at how Yoga extends beyond the mat and becomes a way of living: through awareness, discipline, practice, and understanding. Led by Prashantji
