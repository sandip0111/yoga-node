Now that you are part of this community, we’d like to share a little more about who we are and the heart behind Yoga Vidya School.

Yoga Vidya is not just a school — it is a living tradition. Rooted in the ancient wisdom of the Himalayas, our mission is to make these teachings alive, practical, and deeply transformative for modern seekers around the world.

At the center of this vision is Prashant, founder and main teacher of Yoga Vidya School. Born and trained in Rishikesh, the birthplace of Yoga, he carries the teachings of traditional Hatha and Ashtanga, philosophy, pranayama, meditation, and mantra. Prashant is not a monk, and does not see himself as a guru, or a great teacher — but as a simple person, just like you. With a family and many responsibilities, he understands the challenges of modern life and how hard it can be to keep balance.

His path has taken him to study and live in ashrams such as the Bihar School of Yoga and Rishikesh, and he continues to deepen his research through a PhD in yogic sciences. For over a decade, he has been creating and leading educational projects at Yoga Vidya School, helping thousands of students transform their lives through authentic yogic practices.

Prashant reminds us that Yoga is not about perfection, but about living fully, awakening our inner light, and becoming the master of our own life. ✨

Through our trainings, retreats, and online offerings, Yoga Vidya invites you to discover yoga beyond postures — as a way of life, a journey toward balance, clarity, and self-realization.

We are grateful to have you with us and look forward to sharing this path together. 🌿

With gratitude and light,

Prashant and the Yoga Vidya School Team
