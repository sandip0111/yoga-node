The Pranic Path
Your Journey Through the Pranic Path

At Yoga Vidya School, we have designed a progressive learning journey called the Pranic Path. It is a structured progression that takes you from the foundations of breath awareness to our most advanced Pranayama Certification: Pranic Bodh.

The Pranic Path is divided into different stages, and each one builds upon the previous one. As you progress, the practices become more subtle and advanced, requiring a strong foundation in both knowledge and personal experience.

This is why following the recommended progression is important: each course prepares you for the next, allowing you to deepen your understanding and develop your personal practice step by step.
